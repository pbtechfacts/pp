Accessibility

2. Our Dedicated Commitment to Blind & Visually Impaired Users

Third Eye TTS was conceived to serve as a vital assistive gateway for blind, visually impaired, dyslexic, and low-vision users worldwide. As an assistive tool, we uphold the highest ethical standards:

  • TalkBack Integration: The app registers as an Android system `TextToSpeechService`. When you navigate your phone using TalkBack, Third Eye TTS reads UI elements, messages, and labels purely in memory.
  • Zero Eavesdropping: We do not log what is spoken. Whether reading banking information, personal messages, medical records, or passwords, the engine does not cache, record, or transmit that content.
  • Keyboard & Audio Navigation: Both this website and the Android application comply with WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards, supporting screen readers, braille displays, and large text scales.

Processing

4. Information We Process and How We Use It

Third Eye TTS operates strictly as an Android speech engine. To perform this role, the app processes minimal technical parameters:

Audio Synthesis Text Submitted for Speech Synthesis

When an app (such as TalkBack, an e-book reader, or the Third Eye TTS test interface) requests text to be read aloud, that text string is fed directly to the local acoustic speech model. The generated PCM audio buffer is streamed into Android's `AudioTrack`. As soon as audio finishes playing, the temporary memory buffer is discarded.

Configuration App Preferences & Voice Settings

Settings such as speech rate, pitch, language selection, volume boost, and voice profile are saved solely in the application's private `SharedPreferences` on your local device. These settings are never synchronized with an external server.

Diagnostics Crash Reports and Performance Metrics

If the application crashes, Android's built-in Google Play services may submit an anonymized stack trace to the Google Play Developer Console. This report contains no personal identifiers or spoken text; it contains only code execution lines, device brand/model, and OS version to enable rapid bug fixing.

Android System

6. Android Permissions & Technical Justifications

Google Play Developer Policies require transparent justification for every declared permission in the application manifest. Below is the comprehensive rationale:

android.permission.INTERNET Network Access (Optional)

Purpose: Utilized exclusively if the user requests downloading additional high-definition voice language models or voice updates from our verified CDN/repository, and for Google Play license verification. It is never used to transmit user text or speech output.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE Core Service

Purpose: Required by Android when synthesizing lengthy articles, books, or screen reader queues in the background without being killed by the Android battery optimization manager. Ensures seamless audio continuity for blind users.

android.intent.action.TTS_SERVICE System Engine

Purpose: Standard Android Intent Filter that allows Third Eye TTS to register as a recognized system Text-to-Speech engine in Android Settings > Accessibility > Text-to-speech output.

Scoped Storage / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO User Triggered

Purpose: Only activated if the user explicitly utilizes the optional "Export Speech to Audio File (WAV/MP3)" utility to save a spoken document directly into their device's Music or Downloads folder.

Architecture

7. On-Device Speech Processing Architecture

Unlike cloud-based TTS solutions that transmit your sensitive on-screen text to remote cloud servers for synthesis, Third Eye TTS operates using local on-device neural acoustic models:

1
App or TalkBack Request User encounters text or an accessibility label.
→
2
Local Memory Buffer Text parsed into phonemes in device RAM.
→
3
Acoustic Model Synthesis Native C++/Java synthesizer computes waveform.
→
4
Speaker Output & Wipe Audio streams to speaker; RAM buffer instantly cleared.

* Result: Complete zero-knowledge architecture. No third party, including the app developers, can ever intercept or read the synthesized content.

Data Rights

9. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Under the Google Play User Data Policy, users have the right to request deletion of any personal data.

How to Delete Your Data:

Because Third Eye TTS does not collect personal accounts, email addresses, or store files on remote servers, your data is 100% localized to your Android device. You can purge all application state at any time in under 10 seconds:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Select Apps & notifications (or Installed Apps).
  3. Find and tap Third Eye TTS.
  4. Tap Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and Clear Cache.

This action permanently erases all saved voice settings, local custom pronunciation rules, and cached offline voice models.

Security

11. Security Measures & Technical Safeguards

We employ strict security standards to ensure the safety of our application:

  • Sandboxed Application Storage: All voice configurations and application caches are isolated within Android's private app sandbox (`/data/data/com...`), inaccessible to unauthorized third-party apps.
  • Transport Layer Security (TLS): Any voice pack updates are validated against SHA-256 cryptographic hashes and downloaded via HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
  • Zero Root Privilege Requirements: The app never requests elevated or root privileges and operates entirely within Android's strict permission model.
